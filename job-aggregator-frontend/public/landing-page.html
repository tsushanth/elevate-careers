Frequently Asked Questions

Is it actually free?

Yes. No subscription, no trial period, no credit card required to search or apply.

Where do the job listings come from?

Directly from company career pages via their applicant tracking systems (Greenhouse, Lever, and others) — not scraped or aggregated from other job boards. Every "Apply" link takes you to the company's real application page.

What does the autofill extension actually do?

You save your profile — contact info, resume, common question answers — once in the extension. On a supported application page, it fills in those fields automatically so you don't retype the same information for every application.

Which application sites are supported?

Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, SmartRecruiters, BambooHR, Jobvite, Workable, Recruitee, iCIMS, Taleo, and SuccessFactors. More are added as they're requested.

Is my data secure?

Your saved profile is used only to fill forms on the application pages you visit — it isn't sold or shared. The extension only activates on the specific ATS domains it supports.

Can I track applications I submit elsewhere too?

Yes — the pipeline tracker (Saved, Applied, Phone Screen, Interview, Offer, Rejected) works for any job, whether you applied through SimplyApply's autofill or directly on a company's site.
